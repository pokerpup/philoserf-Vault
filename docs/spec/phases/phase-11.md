# Phase 11 — Magic, gear, companions, fishing

Source: `docs/spec/RPG.md` §12, scope and acceptance criteria copied verbatim (the criteria split at the source's own separators). Session prompt: `docs/spec/phases/phase-11.prompt.md`. Read with `docs/GUARDRAILS.md`.

## Scope

Ledgercraft with all 16 Entries, Ink, the Gilded Reading Room and the Broker, Seals, armor, charms, tempering, Moonsilver forging, Tonics and meals, Juniper and Ada as companions and residents as party members, rods, tackle, bait, the marsh and Vault pools, the fish pond and traps, the first three legendary hunts, C1–C5, M7.

## Acceptance criteria

- (1) every spell casts in the Vault and refuses in safe zones
- (2) a created resident completes C5 as a companion and gains the `lore.memories` entry
- (3) the Broker's three deals each apply the stated status and the stated drop
- (4) The Auditor is catchable only under its stated conditions
