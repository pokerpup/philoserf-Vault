# Phase 9 — Audio, audit and polish

Source: `docs/spec/PROMPT.md` §17, scope and acceptance criteria copied verbatim (the criteria split at the source's own separators). Session prompt: `docs/spec/phases/phase-9.prompt.md`. Read with `docs/GUARDRAILS.md`.

## Scope

Music stems and adaptive layering, SFX, optional voice barks, captions, the Founder's Audit, the Town Ledger, Prestige, Sim Season, accessibility settings.

## Acceptance criteria

- (1) Town Level 1–5 unmutes stems as specified, and a losing week changes the mix as specified and nothing else
- (2) the Founder's Audit scores a fixture save correctly for each lantern tier
- (3) Sim Season runs a 20-minute day on the mock fleet and never mints bridge Marks
