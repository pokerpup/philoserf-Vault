# Phase 9 — Audio, audit and polish

Source: `docs/spec/12-game-layer.md` §10, copied verbatim. Read with `docs/GUARDRAILS.md`.

## Scope

Music stems and adaptive layering, SFX, voice barks, subtitles, the Founder's Audit, the Town Ledger, Prestige, Sim Season, the accessibility settings.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Town Level 1–5 unmutes stems as specified; a losing week changes the mix as specified and nothing else.
2. The Founder's Audit scores a fixture save correctly for each lantern tier.
3. Sim Season runs a 20-minute day on the mock fleet and never mints bridge Marks.
