# Phase 12 — Treasury, Undercount, the Receiver and the Deep Stacks

Source: `docs/spec/RPG.md` §12, scope and acceptance criteria copied verbatim (the criteria split at the source's own separators). Session prompt: `docs/spec/phases/phase-12.prompt.md`. Read with `docs/GUARDRAILS.md`.

## Scope

Wings four and five, the Comptroller and the Receiver, the honest-ledger counters and the Tallies' shield, the third ending, Mister Ink and Marguerite's Trout, Hard Audit, the Deep Stacks with scaling and the lore pages, M8–M10, the secret interactions, Hesper's bounty board.

## Acceptance criteria

- (1) all three endings play from saved games
- (2) the honest-ledger counters change only from the listed in-game choices (a test replays a full real-side day of permission events and asserts zero change)
- (3) Deep Stacks floor 100 is reachable in the sim with Moonsilver gear
- (4) Hard Audit grants no Marks the Standard mode does not
